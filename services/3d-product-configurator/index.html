3D Product Configurators FAQs

What is a 3D product configurator?

It is an interactive 3D view of your product on your website, where customers can rotate it, zoom in and change options such as colour, material or parts, and see the result instantly.

Do I need 3D models of my products?

It helps if you have CAD or 3D files, which we can prepare and optimise for the web. If you do not, we will discuss the best way to create models for your products.

Will it work on mobile phones?

Yes. It runs in the web browser on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and computers, with no app to install.

Can it connect to my online store or quote form?

Yes. A configuration can be sent to your enquiry form, WhatsApp, email, quote system or online store, depending on how you sell.

Is a 3D configurator expensive?

Cost depends on how many products and options you have and whether 3D models already exist. Starting with one key product is a good way to test the impact before adding more.
