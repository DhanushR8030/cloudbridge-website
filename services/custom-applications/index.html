Custom & Desktop Apps FAQs

When is a desktop app better than a web app?

Desktop software is a strong choice when you need speed, offline use, heavy file processing or direct access to hardware such as printers and scanners. We advise on the best format for your case.

Can you rebuild or upgrade our old software?

Yes. We can modernise or replace legacy applications, migrating your data and keeping the business rules your team depends on.

Can the application work without an internet connection?

Yes. Desktop and offline-first applications can work locally and synchronise with a server or cloud service when a connection is available.

Can you build software that connects to hardware?

Yes. We integrate applications with devices such as barcode scanners, receipt printers, scales and other peripherals.
