Mobile App Development FAQs

Should I build a native app or a cross-platform app?

Native apps offer the best performance and deepest device integration. Cross-platform apps share one codebase, which usually means a faster launch and lower cost. We recommend the option that suits your features and budget.

Can you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listings, handle submission and address any review feedback so your app goes live correctly.

Can the app work offline?

Yes. Where it makes sense, we store data on the device and sync when the connection returns, so the app stays useful with poor or no signal.

Do you build the backend and admin panel as well?

Yes. We build the server, database, APIs and an admin dashboard so you can manage users and content.

Should I build for Android or iOS first?

Most smartphone users in India are on Android, so many Indian businesses launch there first. If your customers are mainly abroad or premium buyers, iOS may matter more. A cross-platform build covers both from one codebase.

Do you publish the app on Google Play and the App Store?

Yes. We prepare the store listings, screenshots and submissions, and handle any review feedback until the app is approved and live.
