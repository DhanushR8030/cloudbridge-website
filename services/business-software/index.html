Business Software FAQs

What is custom business software?

It is software built specifically for your organisation’s processes, rather than a generic product you adapt to. It can be a web system, a mobile app, a desktop program or a combination.

Can you replace our spreadsheets and manual processes?

Yes. Replacing spreadsheets and paper-based work with a proper system is one of the most common reasons businesses come to us.

Can the software integrate with tools we already use?

In most cases, yes. We connect with accounting packages, payment gateways, messaging platforms and other systems through their APIs.

How do you keep our business data secure?

We apply role-based access, encrypted connections, secure authentication and regular backups as standard, and we discuss any specific compliance requirements at the start.
