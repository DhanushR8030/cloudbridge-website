Why CloudBridge

Performance is part of the build, not an afterthought.

Most Three.js sites look great on a developer's laptop and stutter on a customer's phone. We plan for the slowest device first.

  • Adaptive qualityThe scene measures the device and its frame rate, then scales detail up or down automatically.
  • GPU-side animationMovement and morphing are calculated in shaders, keeping the main thread free for taps and scrolling.
  • Content first, 3D secondText and buttons load immediately; the 3D code loads afterwards, so search engines and visitors are never kept waiting.
  • Light fallbacksWeak devices, Data Saver users and visitors who prefer reduced motion get a calm static version.

Questions

Three.js & WebGL Development FAQs

Are you a Three.js development company?

Yes. CloudBridge Technologies builds interactive 3D websites and web apps with Three.js and WebGL, from single 3D sections to full experiences, for clients in Coimbatore, across India and worldwide.

Can you work with our existing website or design team?

Yes. We can add a Three.js scene to an existing website, or build the 3D part of a project while your team handles the rest of the design and development.

Can you make an existing Three.js site faster?

Usually, yes. Common fixes include reducing draw calls, moving animation into shaders, compressing models and textures, loading 3D after the main content and adding lighter versions for weaker devices.

Will the 3D hurt our Google ranking?

No, if it is built properly. All important text stays in normal HTML that Google can read, and the 3D loads after the content so page speed scores stay high. Our own site scores 97 to 99 on Google Lighthouse for mobile.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide, with written scope, regular demos you can open on your own devices and full ownership of the source code.
