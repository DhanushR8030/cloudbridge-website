Questions

POS Software FAQs

Do you build POS software for restaurants?

Yes. We build restaurant POS software with table management, kitchen order tickets, takeaway, online orders and recipe-based stock, set up around how your restaurant works.

Can the POS work with my printer and barcode scanner?

Yes. We set up receipt printers, kitchen printers, barcode scanners and cash drawers as part of the project.

Can I use the POS at more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Multiple counters and branches can share the same products, stock and reports, and you can see everything from one place.

Will it work if the internet goes down?

We can design your POS to keep billing without internet and sync automatically when the connection comes back.

Is POS software different from billing software?

A POS is billing software made for the counter, with extras like tables, kitchen orders, cash drawers and shift reports. If you mainly send invoices to other businesses, our billing software may fit better.
