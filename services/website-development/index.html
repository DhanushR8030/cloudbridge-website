Website Development FAQs

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes a few weeks from approved design to launch. Larger sites, online stores and web applications take longer. We confirm a realistic timeline after discussing your requirements.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on real phones, tablets and desktops, because most visitors and ad clicks now come from mobile devices.

Can you connect the website to Google Analytics and Google Ads?

Yes. We set up analytics, conversion tracking and tag management so you can measure enquiries and see exactly which campaigns bring them.

Can I update the website content myself?

Yes. We can build the site on a content management system so you can edit text, images, products and blog posts without a developer.

Do you build 3D or animated websites?

Yes, this is one of our specialities. We build 3D, WebGL-based websites with scroll-driven animation, tuned to stay fast and smooth even on older phones and laptops. The site you are looking at right now is an example of that work.
