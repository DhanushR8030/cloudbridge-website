About CloudBridge Technologies

CloudBridge Technologies is a software development company in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We build websites, 3D websites, Android and iOS apps, business software and desktop applications for businesses in India and around the world.

Who we are

Built in Coimbatore.

CloudBridge was started in Coimbatore to give businesses software that is built properly from the first line of code.

We have delivered 100+ projects across websites, mobile apps, desktop software, business systems and secure access control, for clients in hospitality, construction, manufacturing, automotive, infrastructure and automation.

Every client works directly with the developer actually building their software. There are no account managers in between, so decisions are quick and nothing gets lost in translation.
