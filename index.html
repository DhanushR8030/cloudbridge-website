Questions

Good to know before we start.

What does CloudBridge Technologies do?

We design and build websites, Android and iOS mobile apps, desktop software and custom business systems for startups and established businesses.

Do you build both Android and iOS apps?

Yes. We build for Android and iOS, either as separate native apps or as a single cross-platform app, depending on what suits your product and budget.

How long does a project take?

A business website typically takes a few weeks. Mobile apps and custom software take longer depending on scope. After a short discovery conversation we give you a realistic timeline.

How much does a website or app cost?

Cost depends on scope, features and timeline. Once we understand your requirements we provide a written estimate so you know what to expect before work begins.

Will my website be optimised for search engines and ads?

Yes. Every site we build has clean semantic structure, fast loading, mobile-first design, proper metadata and structured data, and can be connected to Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support after launch, including updates, monitoring, fixes and new features as your business grows.
